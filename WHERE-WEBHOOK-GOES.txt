Discord webhook does NOT go in this zip.

Put it here only:

1. dash.cloudflare.com
2. Workers & Pages -> tvwebhook
3. Settings -> Variables and Secrets -> Add
4. Type: Secret
5. Variable name: DISCORD_WEBHOOK
6. Value: your Discord webhook URL
7. Deploy

In script.js only change STATS_URL to your workers.dev link
(example: https://tvwebhook.abc123.workers.dev)

That URL is public on purpose. The webhook is not.
