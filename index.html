FAQ & Help

What is TvMenu Lib?

TvMenu Lib is a custom development library created specifically for building and running mod menus in Gorilla Tag fan games and copy projects. It's completely free to download from GitHub.

Are the Modded APKs safe to use?

Yes, all custom application modifications provided are tested for Quest. However, always use modded clients responsibly according to game terms.

When is TvMenuPCVR releasing?

TvMenuPCVR is currently undergoing testing and development. Join the community Discord server to keep track of progress updates and release dates.

How do I get support or contact you?

You can reach out directly via email at TvmanMods@proton.me or open a support ticket in the official Discord community server.
